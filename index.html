How to order

Five steps, zero stress.

From checkout to your doorstep, here's the whole journey. Most of it happens in one easy chat with our team.

Start an Order

  1. Choose your specs & check out

    Pick your cover type, cover colour, orientation and number of pages, then place your order.

  2. Fill in the order form

    Right after checkout, we send you a form via chat. Drop your order details there.

  3. Send your best shots

    Group your photos by page in Google Drive and email us the link. The steps are right below.

  4. Finalise the design

    Our team lays out your book, then you review and approve the final design. One free revision included.

  5. Production & delivery

    Production takes 5–7 days. Delivery time depends on the courier you choose.

Grouping photos

How to group your photos

One folder per page, so your story flows in the exact order you want. Here's how on the Google Drive app.

  1. 1

    Tap the + button

    Open Google Drive and tap + at the bottom right.

  2. 2

    Choose Folder

    From the menu, tap Folder.

  3. 3–4

    Name it & create

    Write your folder name, then tap Create.

  4. 5

    One folder per page

    Inside it, make Page 1, Page 2 and so on. Put 2–4 photos in every page.

If your photos exceed the photo capacity, we'll only use the maximum number of photos allowed for the number of pages you ordered.
Photos that aren't grouped into folders will be arranged at random.
Sending photos

How to send your photos

Folders ready? Open up access, copy the link and send it our way.

  1. 1

    Open the folder menu

    Inside your main folder, tap ⋮ at the top right.

  2. 2

    Tap Manage access

    Pick Manage access from the menu.

  3. 3

    Tap Change

    Under General access, tap Change.

  4. 4

    Tap Restricted

    Tap Restricted to open the options.

  5. 5

    Choose Anyone with the link

    Select Anyone with the link so our team can open it.

  6. 6

    Copy the link

    Tap the link icon. You'll see “Copied”.

7

Email us the link

Paste the copied link into an email to us, using this format.

To: binnerdesainer@gmail.com
Subject: FILE_FOTO_[Order ID]
Body: [paste your Google Drive link]
Send photos in original quality. Screenshots and photos re-sent through chat apps lose detail.
